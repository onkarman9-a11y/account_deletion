Account Deletion

EduBridge Education Consultancy respects your privacy. Users of our Invoice Extraction platform can request deletion of their accounts and associated personal information.

1. How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your account, send us an email using your registered email address. Mention "Account Deletion Request" in the subject.

2. Information That Will Be Deleted

After verifying your request, we will delete eligible information associated with your account, including:

  • Your registered account information.
  • Your name and email address.
  • Your account-related personal information.
  • Your task submission records, where eligible for deletion.

3. Information Retention

Certain information may be retained where required by applicable laws or legitimate legal obligations.

4. Deletion Processing

We will review your request, verify your identity and process eligible deletion requests. We aim to complete the process within 30 days.
